Relay

Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Relay is a free app that helps people at a fire department trade shifts with each other. This page explains what Relay stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What Relay stores

  • Your account: your email address (used to send sign-in codes), your first and last name, your phone number, and an optional profile photo or badge colour.
  • Your departments: which departments you belong to, your role (member, admin or owner), and your shift.
  • Schedules: the department's shifts and duty calendar, if an admin sets one up.
  • Trades: the days you post, the days you offer, notes you add, and what happens to each trade (agreed, approved, cancelled, worked).
  • Notifications: a device token so Relay can send you notifications, and your notification choices.
  • Department logos that admins upload.

Who can see it

  • Only people in the same department. Nothing you share in one department is visible to another.
  • People in your department see your name and shift, and the requests and offers you post, including any notes.
  • Your phone number is shown to the other person only once you've both agreed a trade.
  • Department admins can see the members list and approve or decline trades and new members.

What Relay doesn't do

  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your information with anyone for marketing.
  • No tracking or analytics tools. Your phone maker may send Relay crash reports if you've allowed that in your phone's settings.
  • Calendar photos and PDFs you upload to set up a schedule are read on your phone. Only the resulting shift days are saved.
  • "Add to phone calendar" saves an event on your phone only, and only when you tap it.
  • Tip Jar payments are handled by Apple or Google. Relay never sees your payment details.

Where it's kept

Relay's data is stored with Supabase, a hosting provider, on servers in the United States. It's sent over encrypted connections, and access is limited to the people in each department as described above.

How long it's kept

  • Trade history: a finished trade is deleted once every day in it is more than six months in the past.
  • Schedules: calendar days more than six months in the past are deleted.
  • Notifications: deleted after 90 days.
  • Backups: the developer keeps a private nightly backup copy of Relay's data, used only to restore it if something goes wrong.
  • Your account stays until you delete it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Me → Delete account. Your open requests and offers are cancelled, you're removed from every department, and your name, email, phone number and photo are removed straight away. Past trades stay in your department's history as "Former member" until they're cleared after six months. Your sign-in record is deleted 30 days later, so a mistaken deletion can still be undone in that time. If you own a department, you'll be asked to hand it over to another admin first.

Children

Relay is for working members of fire departments and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: use Me → Send feedback in the app, or email relay.shift.app@gmail.com.
