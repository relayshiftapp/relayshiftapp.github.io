Delete your Relay account

Last updated November 8, 2026

You can delete your Relay account at any time, in the app or by email. You don't need to reinstall the app to ask by email.

In the app

Open Relay and go to Me → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm.

By email

Email relay.shift.app@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, and include that address in the message. The request is handled within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done.

Request deletion by email

What deleting does

  • Right away: your open requests and offers are cancelled, you're removed from every department, and your name, email, phone number and photo are removed. Anywhere your name appeared, it shows as "Former member".
  • Set trades stay on your crew's schedule, because those days still need covering. Sort them out with your crew first.
  • Finished trades stay in your department's history under "Former member" until they're cleared after six months.
  • After 30 days your account is removed completely. Until then, a mistaken deletion can still be undone by email.
  • Notifications are deleted within 90 days.
  • Kept: feedback you sent and records of any tips you gave. The developer's private nightly backups may also still hold a copy of your data.

If you own a department

You'll need to hand the department to someone else first: Me → Department settings → Transfer ownership. Then delete your account. If you can't, say so in your email.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • Remove your photo or phone number: Me → Edit.
  • Cancel a request, or withdraw an offer, from the trade itself.
  • Leave a department: Me → Leave.

For anything else, email relay.shift.app@gmail.com.
