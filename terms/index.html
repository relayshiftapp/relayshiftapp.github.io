What Relay is

Relay helps people in the same fire department post days they need covered and agree shift trades with each other. Relay is free.

Relay isn't your official schedule

  • Your department's own rules, contract and officers decide which trades are allowed and who works when.
  • The schedule in Relay is a reference. It can be wrong or out of date, and Relay warns rather than blocks.
  • A trade marked set in Relay doesn't replace whatever your department requires to make it official.
  • You're responsible for showing up for the shifts you agree to work.

Your account

  • Use your real name and keep your phone number current, so the people you trade with can reach you.
  • Keep access to your email secure, since that's how you sign in.
  • Department admins can approve or remove members and manage the department's settings.

Be decent

Don't use Relay to harass anyone, post anything unlawful, or get into a department you don't belong to. Admins can remove members, and accounts that misuse Relay can be closed.
